reivew-prototype / design · issue #5 · step 1 · 2026-10-09

Request changes waits for your OK

When the AI wants to request changes, the review waits on the status page instead of going to GitHub. You can post it, drop findings you disagree with, stop the timer or discard it. If you do nothing, it posts by itself after 30 minutes.

step 1: built step 2: written up, not built only REQUEST_CHANGES waits APPROVE and COMMENT post at once

01The problem

Request changes blocks someone's PR, emails them, and goes out under your name.

Before

  • One 🔴 bug or 🟡 risk found by the AI: Request changes posted at once
  • The author is blocked and emailed before you have looked
  • A wrong or silly finding goes out as yours
  • Approvals already had a safety step (a draft for your OK); Request changes had none

Now

  • The review waits on the status page as Needs your OK, and you get a notification
  • You post it, drop findings, take your time, or discard it
  • Busy? It posts by itself after the wait (30 min by default)
  • Approvals and comments are unchanged

02What was asked for

Three needs, settled in the discussion of issue #5.

03How it works

Job states, with the new one in the middle. Nothing reaches GitHub while a job is held.

FromWhat happensTo
postingThe event is not REQUEST_CHANGES: it posts as beforedone
postingWould request changes, the wait is on, not started by hand: posts nothing, notifies youheld, timer set
heldYou press Postreviewed, then posts at once
heldThe timer runs out (and posting is not paused)posting, posts as it is
heldThe timer ran out while the PC slept or the server was offheld again, fresh timer, new notification
heldYou press I'll handle itheld, no timer
heldYou press Discardskipped, "discarded by you"
heldThe author pushes a new commitskipped, "superseded" (quietly)

A released review (by you or by the timer) is never held again. Before it posts, the usual checks still run: PR merged or closed, newer commit, review no longer requested. Any of these means nothing is posted, as before.

04Decisions and why

Settled one by one with the user before building. Q-numbers match the discussion.

#QuestionDecisionWhy
Q1Which reviews wait?Only ones that would Request changesThat is the one that blocks someone. Approvals already have followUp.approve: pending
Q2Where do you decide?On the status page; nothing on GitHub meanwhileA GitHub draft can't do one-by-one, a timer or "I'll handle it" cleanly, and GitHub allows only one draft per PR
Q3Where is the wait set?publish.holdMin as the default, plus a picker on the pageSame pattern as Model, Effort and Style
Q4Timer ran out while the PC slept?Fresh full wait and a new notificationYou get a real chance to look before it goes out the next morning
Q5What are you told?When it starts waiting, and when the timer posts itYou hear when you are needed and when the bot acted without you. A reminder before posting is noise
Q6npm run review by hand?Posts at onceRunning it by hand already means "post this now"
Q7Choices per finding?Step 1: Keep / Drop. Make optional is step 2. No editingEditing needs an editor and format checks: a lot of work. Drop it, then comment by hand
Q8Which findings get choices?Every new finding, nits tooSame buttons everywhere; lets you drop a silly nit
Q9Timer runs out after some choicesPosts with your choices so far; untouched findings post as writtenChoices are saved on click, so nothing you did is lost
Q10No blocking finding left after dropsApprove, the rest as commentsThe rule used everywhere: nits never block
Q11Earlier findings in a follow-upStep 1: no buttonsThey are already on GitHub, so they can't be dropped. Accepting one is step 2
Q12Discard, and turning the wait offDiscard per review; Off in the pickerDiscard covers a plain wrong review. Off is the old behaviour
Q13One wait for all, or per review?One picker in the headerPost and I'll handle it already cover the per-review cases
Q14Wait changed while reviews waitOnly new waits use itOtherwise switching to Off could post five reviews you were halfway through
Q15What does the author see?Only what is posted. A dropped finding leaves no traceThe author only needs to know what to fix
Q16Timer runs out while posting is pausedWaits for Resume, then posts with your choicesPause means nothing leaves until you say so
Q17PR moves on while waitingA new push replaces it quietly. Merged or closed: checked only at post timeSame as before; no extra polling
ScopeToo many moving parts?Build step 1; write step 2 downMake optional touches the approve-or-block rule and what later rounds remember: the riskiest code. Drop covers most of it

07Inside the code

WhereWhat
src/state.tsStatus held; columns hold_until, released (you or timer) and dropped; hold, release, holdForYou, discard, setDropped. Claiming a held job whose time is up marks it released by the timer. Paused posting leaves held jobs alone
src/publisher.tsWith hold set, a review whose event is REQUEST_CHANGES returns {kind: "hold"} after the duplicate and still-relevant checks, so a closed PR never pings you. Not in pending or dry-run mode, never for a forced post, never for a follow-up already left as a draft for you
src/worker.tsAsks to hold unless the job was released or started by hand. Applies your drops before posting: the AI's original stays in result.ai.json; result.json and review.md become what was posted, so later rounds never see dropped findings
src/hold.tsdropFindings (also from a follow-up's ledger) and the wake watch: a 30 s tick; a gap over 3 min means the PC slept, so a timer that ran out before the wake was missed
src/web/*, src/notify.tsButtons, Drop/Keep, the picker, #job-<id> links; "Needs your OK" and "after the wait" notifications

08Step 2: not built yet

A third choice per finding, Make optional: the author still sees the problem, but it doesn't block the PR, and later rounds never ask about it again. It also works on earlier findings that weren't fixed, so you can let a known issue go with one click instead of Discard plus Approve.

Gap in step 1Workaround todayWith step 2
"Real bug, but don't block" (scenarios 12, 13)Drop it and write a comment yourself, or approve by hand on GitHubPosted with a "🔴 bug · non-blocking" label; Approve if nothing else blocks
Follow-up: accept an unfixed earlier bug (16)Discard, then the Approve button: two clicks, and no "not fixed" reply is postedMake optional: one click posts Approve, and the table says "not fixed (non-blocking)"
A bug you let go is checked again next roundDiscard and Approve again if the author pushes once moreAn optional finding is settled: never checked or raised again
Why it waits. Make optional adds a third kind of finding, "posted but not blocking". That changes the approve-or-block rule (mustFix, chooseEvent, decideFollowUp), the text posted on GitHub, and what later rounds remember in the ledger. Build it only if step 1's workarounds turn out to be a nuisance.

Sketch: an optional flag on a finding, set from the page. mustFix and the round-one blocking rule ignore optional findings; the label adds "· non-blocking"; the ledger carries the flag so needsCheck treats it as settled. Earlier open must-fix findings in a follow-up get Keep / Make optional (no Drop: they are on GitHub already).
